The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date like 7/4 is read as 4 July, so bookings, invites and reminders land on a day with day and month swapped. Only dates with both parts at 12 or below can flip, which is why some months look fine. Reports come from every tenant and every channel.

hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants on booking-v3 who write the day first get bookings through that form typed month first, as the hint asks, and read the tenant’s way. The symptom is the same swap, so subject lines cannot tell it apart; only form bookings at booking-v3 tenants have it. Fixing the parser does not fix the hint. Intake tags were guessed from the reporter’s browser and disagree with the cause in both directions, as the tagged as column shows; do not triage on them.

one-day: dates move by one day. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A day/month swap cannot move a date by one day, or send an invite before the booking was made. These read like a timezone conversion, not a parse. The desk already listed them in desk-notes.json.

For the desk

  • Triage date reports on tenant and booking path, not subject. causes.ts already knows how.
  • Keep a report in the one-day list when it moves a date by a day, not a month.
  • Decision 0001 merged these three into one ticket. It needs a new decision record to replace it.
